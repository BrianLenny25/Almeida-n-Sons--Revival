Our Story

Almeida ’N Sons Revival LLC was born from a lifelong love of working with my hands, creating something from nothing, and taking pride in a job well done.

Growing up, I watched my father build, repair, and transform things with his own two hands. What others might have seen as worn out, broken, or beyond saving, he saw as an opportunity to create something beautiful.

I spent years learning from him, watching his craftsmanship, and discovering the satisfaction that comes from stepping back and looking at something you’ve built yourself.

Those experiences shaped who I am today.

Starting Almeida ’N Sons Revival is my way of carrying those lessons forward while building something of my own. It’s an opportunity to put my skills to work, serve my community, and create a future for my family.

But more importantly, it’s something I hope to share with my son.

I want him to experience what I did growing up. To watch, learn, get his hands dirty, and discover the pride that comes from turning an idea into something real. And maybe, one day, he’ll carry this business forward with the same passion.

I’m not here to build the biggest company or become a millionaire. I’m here to do work I love, provide for my family, treat people fairly, and create something we can all be proud of.

Because at the end of the day, this business isn’t just about the homes we improve.

It’s about the values we build, the people we help, and what we leave behind.

Profit matters. Growth matters. But neither gets to eat the people.
